The Destructibles and Doodads trees group their objects under the World Editor's labels, Trees/Destructibles, Bridges/Ramps, Pathing Blockers, Props and the others, instead of the raw category codes (`D`, `B`, `P`, `O`), and the field table's Category (`bcat`, `dcat`) shows the same label. The labels come from `[DestructibleCategories]` and `[DoodadCategories]` of the install's `UI/WorldEditData.txt`. The Category field is still edited as text.
