A tab of another Object kind shows its own kind on the right: the object last open in that kind, else "Choose an object.", and the URL matches it (`/objects/<rawcode>?kind=<kind>`, or `/objects?kind=<kind>` with no object open). It no longer keeps the object of the last kind beside the tree of the new one, and the browser's Back goes through the tabs as they were shown.
